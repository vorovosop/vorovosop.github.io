VOROVO POS

A point-of-sale program for Windows, made for the VOROVO shop.

What it does

  • Sales at the till: scanning products, receipts, returns and refunds.
  • Products, stock, deliveries and stock counts.
  • Shifts, cash counts and reports for the shop owner.
  • Everyone signs in with their own PIN; cashiers see only the screens they need.

Where the shop's data is kept

All of the shop's data is kept on the shop's own computer, in an encrypted database. VOROVO POS does not send the shop's data to VOROVO or to anyone else.

Backups to Google Drive

If the shop owner chooses to, VOROVO POS keeps encrypted copies (backups) of the shop's database in the owner's own Google Drive, in a folder called "VOROVO POS Backups". The program can see only the files it created there, nothing else in the Drive. How this works, and how to remove the access, is explained in the privacy policy.
